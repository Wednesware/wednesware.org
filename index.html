Get Featured

Got a Sodium publication you want to share? Apply to have your publication featured on the Wednesware homepage! Even non-team members are welcome to apply.

Feature Guidelines

To be featured, your publication must meet the following criteria:

  • Must be available and verified on Sodium.

  • Must be open-source and publicly available.

  • Must be free to use and distribute.

  • Must not contain malicious or harmful content.

  • Must not violate any copyright or intellectual property rights.

  • Must not be named after a chemical element.
